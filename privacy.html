Privacy Policy

Game: The Blackout War (iPhone and iPad)
Publisher: Wright Family Publishing LLC
Contact: wrightfamilypublishing@gmail.com
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what information the game uses and who sees it. The short version: your game saves stay on your device, we do not ask for your name or email in the game, and the only data that leaves your device is gameplay statistics (not linked to your name or email), ad requests, and a request for a small public settings file.

1. What stays on your device

Your pilot callsign, your three save slots, your cash, your weapons, and your settings are stored only on your device. We do not have a copy. If you delete the game, this data is deleted with it. There is no account and no cloud save in this version.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear during a mission, and the first missions are ad-free. You can also choose optional ads that give a reward (for example a bonus payout, one revive, or a daily crate).

  • If you allow tracking when Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt appears, AdMob and its ad partners may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that fit your interests and to measure ads.
  • If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form appears before personalized ads and lets you accept or decline. You can change your choice later by using the Privacy choices option in the game's settings.

AdMob collects some device information (such as your IP address, device type, and ad interactions) to deliver and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

To balance and improve the game, it sends events to GameAnalytics, such as: mission started, completed, or failed; how long a mission took; difficulty chosen; cause of death; cash earned and spent; buying and selling in-game weapons with in-game cash; buying "Remove Ads"; which ads were viewed; story scenes skipped; and session length.

These events are not linked to your name or email, and we do not send your callsign. GameAnalytics's iOS software also sends:

  • your device's vendor identifier (an ID Apple gives to all apps from the same publisher on your device), which it uses as your user ID;
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt, along with whether you allowed it;
  • device details such as the device model and the iOS and game versions;
  • your IP address, which GameAnalytics uses to work out your approximate location (country or area). GameAnalytics says it does not store the full IP address with event data.

Because these identifiers belong to your device, this data is better described as pseudonymous than anonymous. See GameAnalytics' privacy information.

4. Purchases (Apple)

The game has one optional in-app purchase, "Remove Ads". Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your card number, Apple ID, or other payment details. Apple tells the game only whether the purchase is valid, and "Restore Purchases" asks Apple to confirm it again.

5. The settings file

The game downloads a small public file from this website that tells it how often to show ads. Like any web request, this lets the website host (GitHub Pages) see your IP address. The file contains no personal information, and the game works with built-in defaults if it cannot be reached.

6. Part Two email sign-up (optional)

The game may link to a sign-up page for news about Part Two. That page is run by Mailchimp (Intuit Mailchimp), not by the game. If you enter your email there, Mailchimp handles it under its own privacy policy, and you can unsubscribe at any time. The game itself does not collect your email address.

7. Children

The Blackout War is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at wrightfamilypublishing@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

8. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your device: delete the game to remove your saves and settings.
  • Analytics: because the data is not linked to your name or email, we usually cannot tell which events are yours. If you want us to try to have analytics data for your device deleted, write to wrightfamilypublishing@gmail.com and tell us roughly when you installed and played the game.
  • Ads: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS Settings, and change the tracking permission for this game at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be described in the game's update notes.
